# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers of a personal Astro blog who arrive to browse long-form essays, short thoughts, book notes, schedules, and personal archive material in a calm reading context.

## Product Purpose

The site presents a personal writing archive with a dark, refined, literature-forward reading experience. Success means readers can move between essays and short "偶得" notes without visual clutter or layout friction.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, thoughtful, editorial. The interface should feel restrained and deliberate, with enough personality to reflect the author's voice without overwhelming the text.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard density, heavy sidebars on reading-focused short notes, overdecorated glass panels, loud gradients, and card-heavy layouts that make "偶得" feel like an app widget instead of a reading sequence.

## Design Principles

- Let writing lead: typography, spacing, and rhythm should carry the experience.
- Keep context where it helps: long article and archive pages may retain sidebars, while "偶得" should be single-column and focused.
- Preserve the existing dark blog identity while reducing visual noise on reflective pages.
- Use one accent color with restraint for state, navigation, and timeline emphasis.
- Make filtering and navigation useful, but visually subordinate to reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast in dark and light themes, keep keyboard-focus states visible, support reduced motion preferences, and avoid layouts that depend on hover-only discovery.
